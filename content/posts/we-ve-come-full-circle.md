History likes to repeat itself and it does again. I would never expect that office macros will be in the spotlight, especially that Microsoft intentionally (and rightly!) started killing them some time ago. That's what AI Agents are - they are macros, just waaaay worse.

## Past (that can't let go)

I've been working in (business) automation for about 10 years. My journey started as university student when I wanted to create report templates that are automatically calculated based on empirical data gathered during labs. All calculations were done using Microsoft Excel and when I learned that it had capabilities to create reusable scripts ...

I think that macros were crucial for building Microsoft's Office monopoly. It allowed for any custom operations to be build and that's where big companies strive - by doing anything their own way.

Few years ago I decided that all automations should be developed on RPA platform (one we were already using).
